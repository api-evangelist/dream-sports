— By Hardik Parmar , Kunal Chavan , Rupesh Urmaliya Introduction Every day on the Dream11 app, we host thousands of contests so our 110+ million fantasy sports fans can engage with their favorite sports in the palm of their hands. Since the team lineups are usually announced around 30 minutes before the real-life match begins, many of our users choose to join contests moments before the match start time. This is where the need for an optimum user experience becomes crucial for our success.
