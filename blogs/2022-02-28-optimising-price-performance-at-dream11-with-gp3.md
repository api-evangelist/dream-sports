~By Siddharth Terse & Parth Ingole Introduction Amazon Web Services (AWS) announced their next generation general-purpose Solid State Drives (SSD) volumes in December 2020, aka GP3. In the previous version GP2, high throughput and Input/Output Operations Per Second ( IOPS) were required for better performance, and a large storage or IOPS volume was needed to be provisioned. This usually led to the wastage of storage resources at much higher costs.
